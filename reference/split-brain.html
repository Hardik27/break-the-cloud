Solid cells are committed. Dashed cells are written but not yet committed and can still be thrown away. Colour = term.

Raft by Diego Ongaro and John Ousterhout (USENIX ATC 2014). This is a faithful small model: real terms, votes, log matching and majority commit, with message delays you can watch. The safety monitor checks every commit on every server. If it ever shows a violation, the bug is in this page, not in Raft.
